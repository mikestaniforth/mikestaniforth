## What I'm Building

I work at the point where filmmaking, software, and applied AI meet. My background is cinematography, so I tend to build tools around real production problems: faster concepting, clearer visual development, better client communication, and repeatable creative workflows.

Recently, my focus has been on AI-powered systems for Vertical Haus and related creative projects:

- **AI image and video generation tools** for internal creative development and production workflows
- **Previsualisation and storyboard systems** for shaping film ideas before the shoot
- **Creative director and quote-generation assistants** for faster client-facing production planning
- **Chatbots and booking assistants** that turn useful domain knowledge into practical interfaces
- **Social asset and onboarding generators** for repeatable brand and campaign work
- **Portfolio and client websites** built with modern web frameworks and deployment workflows

## How I Work

I like tools that are practical, visual, and production-aware. The best software for creative teams should reduce friction without flattening the creative process, so I care about interfaces that are clear, systems that are reliable, and AI that earns its place in the workflow.

My work usually starts with a creative bottleneck: a slow handover, a repetitive production task, a client communication gap, or a visual idea that needs to be explored faster. From there I build small, focused systems that help people move from idea to execution.
